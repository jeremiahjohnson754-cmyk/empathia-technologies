# Confidentiality classification

Generated by `scripts/build_vault.py` from `scripts/classification.json` (edit the JSON, then re-run). Every monorepo file inherits the class of its longest matching path; the per-file result is in `source-manifest/SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| PUBLIC | may be published |
| INVESTOR_DISCLOSABLE | may be shown to investors once its distribution is authorized for that channel |
| CONFIDENTIAL_TECHNICAL | engineering material; authorized technical reviewers under confidentiality |
| INTERNAL_ONLY | the founder and the internal team only |
| RESTRICTED_IP | core know-how, algorithms and IP records; need-to-know, founder and counsel |
| THIRD_PARTY_LICENSED | governed by someone else's licence or terms |

Classification decides who may see a thing; it does not authorize distribution. No item is authorized for external distribution today, and **no part of the source vault is INVESTOR_DISCLOSABLE**. Nothing is designated PUBLIC.
