# Chain-of-title checklist

For review by qualified counsel. Each row says what evidence exists in this workspace (by path), what is missing, and a status:

- **EVIDENCE_PRESENT** — the workspace holds records that bear on the item (not proof of title).
- **FOUNDER_TO_SUPPLY** — only the founder can provide the missing facts or documents.
- **COUNSEL_REVIEW_REQUIRED** — a legal judgement is needed whatever the evidence.

Nothing in this checklist establishes ownership. Where an item could be subject to another party's rights, ownership is **not** asserted.
