# AI-assisted development record

Factual record for counsel and diligence. It states what the repositories and files show, and marks what they cannot show. It draws no conclusion about authorship, copyright, ownership or protectability; those are questions for qualified counsel.

## Summary

The software, documents and most media-production tooling in this workspace were **substantially generated by an AI system (Claude, made by Anthropic) working in Claude sessions under the founder's direction**. The founder set the scope, requirements, priorities, constraints and acceptance decisions through written directives and owner decisions; Claude wrote the code, tests, documents and commit messages, ran the builds and tests, and recorded the results. This program's own outputs (the monorepo, this vault) were produced the same way.

Nothing in the repositories evidences human-written source code. If the founder or anyone else wrote code, specifications, designs or media by hand, that evidence is outside these repositories and is listed below as **FOUNDER_TO_SUPPLY**.
