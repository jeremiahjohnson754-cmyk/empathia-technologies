# Commissioning test catalogue

Generated by `scripts/docs/test-catalog.mjs` from the engine's data (`packages/domain-data`); do not edit by hand. 33 base tests and 61 discipline-pack tests in 11 packs.

Which tests apply to a room is decided by the engine (`packages/commissioning-engine/src/applicability.ts`) from the room's archetype, devices, tier and risk; each applicable row on a room states why it applies. **Waivable: no** means no exception, waiver or deferral may carry a failure of that test (safety, security, evidence integrity and separation of duties are among them). Methods describe the field practice in the product's own words; no standard is reproduced.

Phases (the engine's names; the demonstration's room pages show them as Readiness, Physical QA, Infrastructure and platform, Functional, Operational and experience): 1 Pre-install / survey readiness · 2 Installation and documentary trace · 3 Integrated validation (Tier 1 / 2 / 3) · 4 Post-commission, deficiencies, acceptance readiness · 5 Final room photography, document reconciliation, handoff release; sign-off follows.
