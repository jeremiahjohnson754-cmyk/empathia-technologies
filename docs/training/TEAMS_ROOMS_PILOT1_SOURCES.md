# Pilot 1 (everyday collaboration): the documented facts it may teach

Status: **documentation identified** (the second of the verification states). Not scripted, not reviewed, no media. Read 12 Nov 2026.

Source: Microsoft, [Teams Rooms (Windows)](https://support.microsoft.com/office/microsoft-teams-rooms-windows-e667f40e-5aab-40c1-bd68-611fe0002ba2)
(page metadata: updated 24 Sep 2026) and [home screen and admin controls](https://learn.microsoft.com/microsoftteams/rooms/mtr-home-refresh).
Applies to: Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows. Android has its own guide and is out of scope for this pilot.

Facts, paraphrased, each to be checked again by a technical reviewer before a script is approved:

1. **Join a scheduled meeting:** the meeting appears on the room console only if the room was invited; select **Join**. Other ways in:
   invite the room, the room's QR code, **Join with an ID**, or join on a personal device and pick the room for audio (turn the personal
   device's audio off to avoid echo).
2. **Start a meeting now:** **Meet now**, then invite people by name.
3. **Share a laptop:** connect the room's cable; sharing may start by itself (an administrator setting) or select **Share**. Stop with
   **Stop sharing**. Wireless casting has its own article and is not part of this pilot.
4. **Microphone and volume:** from the meeting controls.
5. **Leave:** **Leave** from the meeting controls.

Variations a lesson must state, or ask the room's record about: automatic HDMI sharing (administrator setting); a touch display is
needed for Whiteboard; room controls exist only where an administrator installed a manufacturer's app; some features need a Teams Rooms
Pro licence; one or two front displays.

Not stated by the sources, so a lesson must not claim it: how whiteboard content is saved; how to end a meeting for everyone.
