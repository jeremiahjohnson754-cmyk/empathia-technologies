# Pilot 1 script, draft 1: joining and running a meeting in a Teams Rooms on Windows room

Status: **script drafted**, awaiting technical review against `TEAMS_ROOMS_PILOT1_SOURCES.md`. Not storyboarded, no media. 12 Nov 2026.
Format: immediate task guide, 60–75 seconds, narrated, captioned. Audience: everyday users; no engineering terms.
Applies to: Teams Rooms on Windows with a touch console. The lesson card shows this before it plays, and Lite shows it only where the
room's asset record says the room has that system (never guessed from a photograph).

Visuals: an original, generic console and front display, labelled on screen "Illustration, not the actual screen". Control names in
bold are the words Microsoft's article uses for the buttons. No Microsoft logo, icon or screenshot.

| # | On screen | Narration (draft) | Source fact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The room; the console on the table | "Here's how to run your meeting from the console on the table." | — |
| 2 | Console home; a meeting on the list | "If your meeting invited this room, it's on the console. Tap **Join**." | 1 |
| 3 | No meeting listed | "Not there? The room has to be invited. You can also use **Join with an ID**, or scan the room's QR code." | 1 |
| 4 | A laptop beside the console | "Joining from your laptop too? Turn its audio off, so the room doesn't echo." | 1 |
| 5 | **Meet now** | "No meeting booked? Tap **Meet now**, then add people by name." | 2 |
| 6 | Cable into the laptop | "To share your screen, plug in the room's cable. In some rooms it shares by itself; if not, tap **Share**." | 3 + variation |
| 7 | **Stop sharing** | "When you're done, tap **Stop sharing**." | 3 |
| 8 | Meeting controls: microphone, volume | "Mute and volume are on the meeting controls." | 4 |
| 9 | **Leave** | "To finish, tap **Leave**." | 5 |
| 10 | The room's help card | "Something not working? Use this room's help card to reach support." | Empathia's room record (owner from the record) |

Deliberately left out (the sources don't say it): how to end the meeting for everyone; saving a whiteboard; wireless casting (its
own article; a later lesson).

Practice step (interactive player, after step 3): "Your meeting isn't on the console. What do you do?" Choices: *Tap Meet now and
wait* · *Use Join with an ID* · *Restart the console*. Correct: *Join with an ID*; feedback names why (the room wasn't invited, and an
ID joins the meeting itself; Meet now starts a different meeting; restarting doesn't add the meeting).

Review checklist for the technical reviewer: each step against the source; the variations stated; nothing the source does not say;
plain language; length; caption text equals narration.
