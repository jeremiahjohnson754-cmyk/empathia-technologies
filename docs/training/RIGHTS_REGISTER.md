## What this means for the first pilots

- A training film can teach a vendor's documented steps in original words, with an original, clearly generic illustration of the
  controls and a link to the vendor's article. It cannot show the vendor's interface as if it were the real screen unless that vendor's
  terms or a written permission allow it, recorded here first.
- "Device-accurate" therefore means: every step and control name matches the vendor's current documentation for the stated product and
  configuration, the variations the documentation names are stated, and the review date is shown. It does not mean pixel-identical UI.
