# The 90-room Enterprise estate

Generated by `node scripts/docs/enterprise-90.mjs` from the campus canon and the room records the engine builds from it. Every count is a
sum of records. Everything here is **fictional and synthetic**: no real organisation, building, person or measurement.

> Since 13 November 2026 the estate also holds 50 of V2.5.3's own rooms after these 90 (140 in all), taken as V2.5.3 shows them; they are listed in `apps/demo/src/campus/canon.ts` (V253_ROOMS). This record stays the founder's 90.

## What changed

The Enterprise world held 45 rooms. The founder's 90-room roster proposes 45 more, nine sectors of five, to be de-duplicated against
the first 45. **The estate now holds 90 rooms**: the first 45 with their ids, geometry and histories untouched, and the 45 additions as rows
46 to 90. Each addition is designed by the commissioning engine from an archetype it already knows, exactly as the first 45 were:
deterministic, seeded, device classes only (no make, model or serial), dimensions and capacity MODELED. No two additions share their
dimensions, and none shares them with one of the first 45.

| | Rooms | Device positions | Where the records stand | Sizes |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| First 45 | 45 | 541 | 36 accepted, 7 commissioning, 1 operational, 1 planned | 8 of up to 8 seats, 31 of 9 to 40, 6 larger |
| Additions | 45 | 607 | 39 accepted, 6 commissioning | 11 of up to 8 seats, 27 of 9 to 40, 7 larger |
| **Estate** | **90** | **1148** | 75 accepted, 13 commissioning, 1 operational, 1 planned | 19 of up to 8 seats, 58 of 9 to 40, 13 larger |

The roster asks for roughly a third small rooms, a third medium and the rest large or specialised. The estate is not that: it is
19 of up to 8 seats, 58 of 9 to 40, 13 larger. The additions were chosen for variety of kind, as the roster says, and the first 45 were not rebalanced.

enterprise and each room has to make sense where it stands.

## Not done

- The roster asks every room to have its own network and power topology, accessibility requirements and customer journey. Each addition has
  its own purpose, scenario, size, seats and designed devices; **topology and accessibility requirements are not modelled per room**.
- 26 of the 45 additions are designed from the nearest template the engine has, and say so on their record. There is no gallery, lobby,
  operations-centre or laboratory template yet.
- The roster's seed stories (8 to 12 named incidents, 5 change requests, 8 projects, 5 budget scenarios, 3 design comparisons) are
  **not** extended to the additions: the service workspace and the programme still hold what they held.
- The additions are not drawn on the campus map and have no 3D art of their own beyond what the engine lays out.
- The corporate website still says the demonstration campus has thirty rooms, in the wording the founder approved. It is not changed here.
- No person has reviewed the 45 room names, scenarios or sizes.
