The same compass, three steps further

V2.5.3 and the films
A drawing compass of three nodes: the hinge and its two points. It also reads as Λ, the lambda Spartans painted on their shields.
The tile
The same square and the same green, on black and bronze. The compass now draws its circle in red ink.
The aspis
The circle becomes the films' lifecycle ring, seven nodes from Request to Improve, on a hoplite shield with Athena's olive crown.
