# Empathia emblem: flagship concepts (2026-11-03, for the founder's choice)

**Status: concepts. Nothing here is used by the website, the demonstration or the application yet.** The founder picks a concept
(LEI_DECISIONS D39); the chosen mark then replaces the shell's square mark and the website's header mark in one slice with its tests.

## Where it comes from
The established mark is the V2.5.3 / film emblem: a drawing compass of three nodes (the hinge and its two points) in a rounded square,
in the teal-green `#45cfc1` (`legacy/source/v253/*offline.html`, `.mark`; the end cards of the two product films). Every concept keeps
that compass. Two things the brief asked for fit it without force:
- **Sparta:** an open compass is the letter Λ, the lambda Spartans painted on their shields; the flagship puts it on a black hoplite
  shield (aspis) with a bronze rim.
- **Athens:** an olive crown at the hinge (Athena's tree) and, as a mascot, Athena's owl.
- **The red:** the compass draws the films' lifecycle ring (Request, Design, Commission, Operate, Support, Learn, Improve: seven
  nodes, the two points the first and last) in red ink, with a drip at the pen. "The only blood we spill is red ink" — the redline.
  The war-paint concept is the literal blood-spill version, for launches, merchandise and internal use.
