**Rights basis.** ElevenLabs' help centre ("Can I publish the content I generate on the platform?") says paid plans include a
commercial licence, except for Beta Services, and that content generated during a paid subscription can be used commercially
indefinitely. Content made outside a paid subscription cannot.

**Rule for this repository.** A generated file may be marked `rightsStatus: VERIFIED` only if:
- it was generated between 2026-11-01 and the end of a paid period on record;
- it was made with a non-Beta model;
- its manifest entry records the provider, plan, `generatedAt`, the voice or model id, `termsUrl`/`termsCheckedAt`, and this file as
  `billingEvidenceRef`.

Later renewals need their own entry here.
