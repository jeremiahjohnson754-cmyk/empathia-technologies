# SIGNAL: An Empathia Learn Story — v1.7 SHOWRUNNER / NARRATIVE & CINEMATICS BIBLE

**Source:** `signal-standalone_ORIGINAL.html` SHA256 recorded in chapter inventory. **This document proposes craft and production improvements; it does NOT replace, edit or recertify the original story.**

## What the show is

A character-first dramatic technical thriller, grounded in real audio, video, networking, systems engineering, communications, cybersecurity, service, design and governance. Professional competence is not magic: the heroes observe, think, ask for authorization, test safely, document and verify. The emotional reward is the restoration of human trust when systems fail. Adult drama and stakes, accessible teaching, relief through humane comedy, and hard-earned character growth can coexist.

**Pitch:** A volunteer learns that every broken system has a human story. From a small sanctuary to a mission-critical enterprise estate, Maya learns how to hear what infrastructure is telling her—and what people are afraid to say.

### The non-negotiable original

Audit of the actual HTML source (November 8, 2026): 155 `<section class="chapter">` sections, split into 11 prologue, 133 season episodes (including S01E00), and 11 legacy `ch` elements; 1,610 `figure.panel` objects, 387 `.decision` elements, 121 quest cards and 260 dialogue-beat blocks. These counts concern structure, not an independently reviewed literary quality score. No source media video/audio elements were found in earlier inspections; producing cinematic masters and voices is new actual production, not a completed fact.

An accompanying `SIGNAL_CANON_CHAPTER_INVENTORY_v1_7.json` records all IDs, titles, detected counts, sample dialogue and beat excerpts for source-preserving migration. It is not a substitute for the 27 MB original HTML.

## A. What made the original powerful — source-specific proof

**S01E00 – Signal / The Stream That Keeps Dying.** At stake: Walter, recovering far away, relies on the stream as his connection to church. Maya has little confidence, Theo means well with a surprise, and Ray won't permit reckless guesses. Forty-five minutes to doors. Editing emphasis: protect Walter's dignity; don't show panicked rebooting as heroism; reveal a technical cause by clue stacking. Comedy can land on a familiar update dialog, a half-remembered number, and an embarrassing yet loving correction.

**S01E01 – Ten Seats / The Hollow Seats.** Dolores fears losing her hearing; an acoustic geometry issue in the same seats has persisted for years. The human injury is much bigger than a tonal mismatch. Protect the dignity of someone worried about a medical problem; a technical repair must not pretend to diagnose their health. Ray's broom-physics humor works because it comes from character and engineering practicality. Emotional payoff: restore seat experience and accountability, not a miracle cure.

**S01E02 – The Muddy Mix.** Maya believes she learned to remove 'mud' until Lily asks WHY and exposes shallow certainty. This is not a generic EQ quiz; it's a story about teaching, humility and measurement. Preserve Dante and Marisol's voices and the lived cost of blaming performers for signal-chain failures.

**S02E01 – Advance.** Tour advance packets disagree, a venue overhead limitation lacks verified rating, and someone wants to reuse last tour's plan. Real touring comedy emerges from exhausted crew humor and deadlines, not from reckless rigging as acceptable practice.

**S04E01 – One Hour to Air.** A chief engineer leaves before a live broadcast; video tears, CAM2 and return issues grow; a trainee is isolated. Missing warning history is a character and institutional failure. Maintain correct reference/genlock/frames language and facilities ownership of unsafe roof work.

**S09E01 – The Remote Account.** A temporary support account remains active for ninety days and was accessed Sunday. The thriller is governance and responsibility; it is not a tutorial for breaking into equipment. Security owner controls investigation; support access is replaced safely with named access and audit, not by cutting off critical service recklessly.

**S12E01 – The Exception Register.** Forty-one differing rooms versus twelve known exceptions. Rashida's ask: leave the enterprise capable of operating without Maya. The culmination of character arc is mentorship, change governance and sustainable ownership rather than lone genius fixes.

## B. Tone compass — action, mystery, dread, love, comedy

**Character humor:** Maya uses dry deflection when uncertain; Ray uses unreasonably precise engineering metaphors; veterans may make jokes about spreadsheets, labels, coffee or familiar mistakes. Comedy should not humiliate beginners, persons with disabilities or customers. No juvenile AI banter in high-stakes scenes.

**Drama:** authentic conflicts between fast restoration and evidence preservation, customer urgency and safe change controls, seniority and better evidence, friendship and accountability, design intent and what the room actually does. Not random yelling.

**Action:** urgent physical problem-solving within professional safety procedures: inspect signal path, move to a safe control location, triage incident, discover a last-resort approved alternative, rehearse a cutover, communicate on headset and verify. No dangerous novice roof climbs, unapproved rigging, loudspeaker blast, physical intrusion or instant magical code fixes.

**Mystery:** seed three candidate explanations, one silent clue, and at least one consequential competing human interpretation. Reveals must be fair on reread. A recurring 'unknown hand' change, institutional neglect or undocumented standard can thread episodes without retroactively changing source canon.

**Love for the profession:** depict good cable dressing, instrument discipline, annotated diagrams, logs, calibrated data and a handoff that another tech can actually use; beauty in method, not in intimidation.

## C. A cinematic episode, beat by beat

Use a flexible structure, not a mandatory formula each time:

1. **Cold open (0:00–0:45)**: character affected, a single impossible observation or human consequence, iconic sound. No generic exposition.
2. **The clock (0:45–2:00)**: name what is at stake, permissions, who will pay for a bad decision.
3. **Competing diagnosis (2:00–4:00)**: plausible rival theories and who believes them.
4. **The clue (4:00–7:00)**: one instrument, signal chain observation or document speaks truth.
5. **The bad shortcut (7:00–9:00)**: pressure to act without knowledge; one authentic funny/awkward beat may release tension.
6. **Turning point (9:00–11:00)**: Maya recognizes the correct question to ask, not an impossible answer.
7. **Interactive choice**: user can reason based on actually presented evidence; branch has consequences, a repair path and after-action explanation.
8. **Verification (time variable)**: silence, meters, headset confirmation and independent evidence; if uncertain, say uncertain.
9. **Aftershock**: feelings, responsibility, consequences, trust and process, sometimes unresolved.
10. **Transfer**: a 2–5 minute simulation or a deeper lab, optional for story-only viewers except paid/institution-authorized mastery paths.

Keep the actual chapter's length and available source panels as controlling; exact minute marks above are provisional production examples, **not canonical run times**.

## D. Source-preserving editorial diff protocol

Script doctor should be *specific*: improve a flat sentence, trim repetitive exposition, reposition a reveal, deepen a supporting character's voice or strengthen an earned silent beat. - UX: 5 real role-specific customer tasks completed at observed target rates; product claims visible and tested; mobile/keyboard/assistive users able to reach core functions.
- SIGNAL: full cinematic test episode approved by founder, narrative editor, technical engineer and animation/audio reviewers with zero open P0/P1 anomalies; source original still available.
- Performance: budget, realistic device class, network class, percentile, concurrency and trace evidence written before claiming improvement.

# Empathia v1.7 — Architecture Decisions, Trade-offs and Verification Gates

| ADR | Decision | Why | Major counter-risk / guardrail | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ADR-001 | Modular monolith + bounded modules first | Faster delivery/reliability than premature distributed services | Encapsulate schema ownership; explicit typed APIs; can extract independent service later | Package import graph and deploy/rollback test |
| ADR-002 | Tenant Control/Data/Experience planes | Authorization and billing separated from mutable customer telemetry | Never trust client chosen tenant/role; compare active context to token grants | Cross-plane security suites |
| ADR-003 | Postgres authoritative system of record | Transactional consistency for assets/approvals | RLS bypass roles and pooled session leakage; db migrations | DB regression and pool stress |
| ADR-004 | Search Party graph as read projection | Relationships searchable without fractured authority | Stale index, RAG leaks, false confidence | Token-scope and staleness tests |
| ADR-005 | Shared workflow engine | Reuse gated approval model across products | Must preserve per-domain validations; no universal magic status | 6 golden paths + negative tests |
| ADR-006 | Shared evidence ledger | One report source and legal chain of custody | Hashes alone don't prove measurement quality | Reproducible source-to-report audit |
| ADR-007 | Outbox + durable workers | Eliminate lost updates in asynchronous flows | Duplicate/reordered events; idempotency/ordering | Fault injection/replay |
| ADR-008 | Connector capability contract | Avoid vendor API overclaims and duplication | Different API entitlements and terms per vendor | Per-adapter contract fixture |
| ADR-009 | Outbound-only edge gateway | Support equipment without public inbound reach | On-prem permits, security review, revocation, TLS | No unapproved ports; enrollment tests |
| ADR-010 | Separate demo/live paths | Proof honesty and customer data protection | Accidentally invoking live through synthetic event | Isolation and actuation proof |
| ADR-011 | QR opaque resolver | Secure access without embedded credentials | Token leakage and unauthorized enumeration | Negative URL tests |
| ADR-012 | Immutable dossier snapshot | Consistent executive artifacts | Export job concurrency; long-form media | PDF/DOCX/XLSX/JSON count comparison |
| ADR-013 | 2D-first Lite with optional 3D | Mobile usability and accessibility | Need accurate geometry and modeling confidence | Timed low-end phone benchmark |
| ADR-014 | Shared Experiences primitives | Reuse across Stadium/University/Convention | Each world still has distinct auth and privacy | Role-specific E2E tests |
| ADR-015 | SIGNAL source-canon content pipeline | Preserve loved story while producing film | AI face/voice drift; new canon hallucinations | Original hash and chapter choice diff |
| ADR-016 | First 3–5 cinematic episodes then scale | Quality before volume | Incomplete rest-of-series media is transparently marked | Human production QC |
| ADR-017 | Hosted Stripe-class provider payment | PCI/payment compliance footprint reduction | No raw banking in app, settlement latency | Webhook/entitlement test |
| ADR-018 | Founder Platform Owner without omnipotent backdoor | Founder business control plus customer trust | Recovery and accountability | MFA break-glass test and audit |
| ADR-019 | Contextual roles ≠ grants | UX and security separation | Default-deny enforcement | 52 contexts negative tests |
| ADR-020 | Failure-first release engineering | Prevent beautiful-but-broken SaaS | Automated tests are not real security certification | Signed evidence and rollback exercise |
