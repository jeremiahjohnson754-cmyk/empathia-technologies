# Historical pricing reconciliation and proposed v1.5 menu

**Do not overwrite the originals** `Empathia_Financial_Model.xlsx` and `Empathia_Profitability_Model.xlsx`. Their assumptions differ materially. A public menu cannot both assume a $1,100/month enterprise Pro base plus $20/room and an unrelated $16,000/year core plus $40/room without choosing a coherent plan.

| Metric | Financial Model (base-case) | Profitability Model (base-case) | Proposed v1.5 public candidate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Core/base | Pro $1,100/month | Core $16,000/year | Core Enterprise $1,099.99/month; Starter $149.99 capped |
| Pro room | $20/month | $40/month | $19.99/room/month |
| Field Intelligence | $24/room/month | $15/room/month, 50% attach | $23.99/room/month |
| Search Party | $0.45/asset/month | $3,000/year | $0.45/asset/month, $49.99 minimum not additive |
| Learn | $22/learner/month | $2,500/year average | Learn enterprise $21.99; SIGNAL individual $24.99; Teams $44.99/seat |
| Lite | $350/building/month | Workplace entitlement $2,500/year | Lite $349.99/building/month |
| Standard onboarding | $8,500/account baseline | $15,000/account baseline | Separately scoped services, low-fee self-serve pilots only |
| Enterprise seat | Not part of original baseline | Not separately modeled | **$9.99 active named staff user / month** |

All rows are planning assumptions, not competitor quotes or tested willingness to pay. Proposed examples and discounts must be tested for margins by cohort: small organizations (<=5 rooms), enterprise AV estates, MSP/reseller partner deployment, university student cohorts, venue/event projects and SIGNAL direct subscribers.

**Required unit economics input:** for each SKU estimate hosting, database and storage, media-CDN bandwidth, LLM tokens, connector/vendor API costs, payment processing, onboarding, engineering support, qualified security costs and sales CAC; document gross margin *per actual unit*, not arbitrary headline. A $49.99 discovery pilot cannot include unlimited engineering labor. Track revenue recognition and outstanding refunds. Price bundles against the actual line-item sum, subtract 5-8% only from eligible software. No duplicate seat or search minimum.

**Subscription cancellation:** the 2024 FTC Click-to-Cancel rule was vacated in July 2025; nonetheless build clear pre-billing consent, easy cancellation and notices per applicable consumer laws. Do not advertise unverified regulatory certifications. Verify payment implementation details with current provider docs and counsel.

**Numbers currently proposed for multi-month SIGNAL:** Individual $69.99/3, $129.99/6, $239.99/12; Teams $129.99/3, $249.99/6, $479.99/12 PER SEAT. Monthly $24.99 individual / $44.99 team seat. Free through S2; $1.99 trial 14 days. Team includes required $9.99 seat; the full published narrative remains preserved in the original source, though filmed content may be a staged later release.
