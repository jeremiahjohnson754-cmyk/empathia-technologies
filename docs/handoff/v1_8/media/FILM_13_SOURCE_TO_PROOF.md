# FILM 13 — ONE ROOM. ONE TRUTH. (Proposed v1.8 flagship)

**Status: SCRIPT/CONCEPT ONLY. No film asserted to exist.**

## Primary narrative
A conference room fails at the worst possible moment. We discover the same asset once, understand its relationships through Search Party, verify the fault against commissioning/Assurance evidence, repair it with proper approval, and generate a dossier showing what was proved, when, and by whom. One persistent asset identity and tenant scope across every shot. We end with a visitor being able to open the sanitized evidence trail and explore an eligible pilot.

## Invariants
1. Room and asset IDs do not magically change between screens.
2. Live/real sources remain real and private; public demonstration uses explicit approved/sanitized derivatives or labeled synthetic fixtures.
3. No automatic green commissioning acceptance when evidence is missing or a deficiency is open.
4. Field technicians and engineer characters use real AV/IT safety process. If the production includes a camera movement, signal check, loudness measurement, network change, DSP or microphone calibration, a qualified reviewer checks physical plausibility.
5. No fictitious customer logos, unauthorized Soldier Field/UIUC/McCormick/Kraft Heinz identity as endorsements, unlicensed VO, or copied broadcaster footage.
6. No unverified claim about revenue, integration coverage, guaranteed time saved or certified security.
7. Original SIGNAL narrative remains intact; this is a separate marketing proof film, not an invented canonical SIGNAL episode.
