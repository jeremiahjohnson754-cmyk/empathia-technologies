Run a meeting from the room console
Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows with a touch console (sources: docs/training/TEAMS_ROOMS_PILOT1_SOURCES.md, read 12 Nov 2026)
Illustration, not the actual screen

Here's how to run your meeting from the console on the table.
If your meeting invited this room, it's on the console. Tap Join.
Not there? The room has to be invited. You can also use Join with an ID, or scan the room's QR code.
Joining from your laptop too? Turn its audio off, so the room doesn't echo.
No meeting booked? Tap Meet now, then add people by name.
To share your screen, plug in the room's cable. In some rooms it shares by itself; if not, tap Share.
When you're done, tap Stop sharing.
Mute and volume are on the meeting controls.
To finish, tap Leave.
Something not working? Use this room's help card to reach support.
